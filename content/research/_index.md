**Yu, R.** CriticalFlow: Dependency-Aware Transport Scheduling for Multi-Agent LLM Systems. *2026 IEEE International Conference on Network Protocols (ICNP)*. (First author, corresponding author). A lightweight transport-scheduling layer that exposes the workflow DAG structure and co-schedules messages by remaining work and critical path to resolve message contention in multi-agent LLM systems.

**Yu, R.** OrbitCC: Ephemeris-Aware Congestion Control for LEO Satellite Networks. *2026 IEEE International Conference on Network Protocols (ICNP)*. (First author, corresponding author). A congestion-control algorithm that leverages ephemeris data to predict geometric RTT, subtracting deterministic geometric delay from measured RTT to accurately recover queuing delay.

**Yu, R.** FrameQUIC: Importance- and Deadline-Aware Media over QUIC. *2026 IEEE International Conference on Network Protocols (ICNP)*. (First author, corresponding author). A QUIC scheduling mechanism for real-time media that senses video-frame importance and playback deadlines, proactively dropping unrenderable frames to free bandwidth.

**Yu, R.** VERDE: Semantic-Aware Weight Adaptation via Large Language Models for Green AI Orchestration. *2026 International Conference on Information and Communications Security (ICICS)*. (Co-first author, corresponding author). An LLM-based cloud-service orchestration framework that optimizes green AI orchestration through semantic-aware weight adaptation to improve energy efficiency and resource utilization.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on the development and application of computational intelligence methods, particularly at the intersection of evolutionary computation, deep learning, and optimization theory. Current research areas include:

**Multi-Objective Combinatorial Optimization**: Development of meta-learning algorithms leveraging evolutionary principles for complex optimization problems with multiple conflicting objectives.

**Deep Generative Models for Optimization**: Integration of generative adversarial networks (GANs) with evolutionary algorithms to enhance population diversity and solution quality in combinatorial optimization.

**Game-Theoretic Optimization**: Application of zero-sum game theory concepts to discrete optimization problems, developing novel algorithmic frameworks.

**Networked & Agentic Systems**: Transport scheduling, congestion control, and QUIC-based media delivery for multi-agent LLM systems, LEO satellite networks, and real-time media, alongside LLM-driven orchestration for green AI.

**Bioinformatics and Computational Biology**: Computational methods for biomarker identification, pathway analysis, and understanding disease progression mechanisms.

**Supply Chain Optimization**: Application of AI and optimization techniques to real-world supply chain challenges, including demand forecasting, inventory management, and logistics optimization.
