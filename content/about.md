## Professional Summary

I am a Senior AI Data Scientist at Procter & Gamble, where I lead the design of multimodal and agentic AI solutions across consumer insight, intelligent marketing, and generative content creation. I build end-to-end AI agent systems—spanning agent memory, skills, and tool integration—and bring deep expertise in machine learning, deep learning, and large-model orchestration. Reduced per-channel integration effort by ~70%, boosted creative throughput ~5×, and saved ~¥4M per year, standardizing a reusable A2A integration and skills platform.

Delivered 240+ marketing strategy recommendations and shortened 12 new-product development cycles by 20%, cutting market-research costs by over ¥35M.
